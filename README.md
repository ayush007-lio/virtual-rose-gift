# 🌹 Virtual Rose Gift

A beautiful and interactive web experience to gift a **virtual rose** with love, glowing visuals, romantic quotes, and background music.  
Perfect for expressing emotions in a simple yet magical way 💖

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌹 **Choose Rose Color**
  - ❤️ Red – Passion & deep love  
  - 🌸 Pink – Sweetness & care  
  - 🤍 White – Pure & eternal love  

- 💬 **Special Love Quotes**
  - Unique romantic quotes based on the selected rose color

- 🌟 **Dynamic Glow Effect**
  - Soft glowing light behind the rose that changes with color selection

- 🎵 **Background Music**
  - Music plays automatically when the rose is given (mobile & desktop friendly)

- 📱 **Fully Responsive**
  - Works perfectly on **mobile, tablet, and desktop**

- ⚡ **Pure HTML, CSS & JavaScript**
  - No frameworks, no libraries — lightweight and fast
